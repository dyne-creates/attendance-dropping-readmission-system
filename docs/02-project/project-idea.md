# Project Idea

## Selected Project

**Attendance, Dropping, Admission, Re-Admission System**

## Project Overview

The project aims to improve the process of managing student attendance, dropping, admission, and re-admission by providing a more organized and consistent system for **faculty members, students, and the Office of Student Affairs (OSA)**.

The system will help reduce manual data entry, improve the consistency of submitted information, provide clearer guidance for students, and help maintain organized student records.

## Identified Issues

### Faculty Perspective

Faculty members from different departments experience difficulties with the current process.

- Some faculty members skip the **dropping process** and proceed directly to re-admission.
- Faculty members manually enter large amounts of information into forms.
- Manual data entry makes the process **tedious and time-consuming**.
- Different processes can result in inconsistent student information.

### Student Perspective

Students who have been dropped experience difficulties when completing the re-admission process.

- Students receive **little to no guidance** regarding the re-admission process.
- Students may need to choose between online and face-to-face procedures without a clear process.
- The current process requires considerable effort to complete.
- Students experience issues with notifications and emails.
- Some students may be unable to retrieve emails from OSA or potentially from their professors.

### OSA Perspective

The Office of Student Affairs experiences difficulties in maintaining consistent student information.

- Online and face-to-face submissions may use different formats.
- Submitted information may lack a consistent structure.
- Forms may not provide sufficient guidance on how information should be entered.
- Inconsistent or improperly formatted data can affect database quality.
- Maintaining records becomes more difficult when information is submitted in different formats.

## Proposed Direction

The proposed system will provide a **centralized and structured process** for managing attendance, dropping, admission, and re-admission.

The system should focus on:

- Standardized information
- Guided form submission
- Centralized records
- Clear re-admission workflow
- Reduced manual data entry
- Consistent data formatting
- Improved communication and notifications
- Better coordination between students, faculty, and OSA
