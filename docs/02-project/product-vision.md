# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

> **To provide a centralized and standardized system that simplifies attendance, dropping, admission, and re-admission processes while improving the accuracy, consistency, and accessibility of student records for students, faculty, and the Office of Student Affairs.**

## Target Users

### Students

The system will help students:

- Understand the required re-admission process.
- Follow a clear step-by-step workflow.
- Submit properly formatted information.
- Track their submissions and application status.
- Receive important notifications.

### Faculty Members

The system will help faculty members:

- Manage student attendance and dropping records.
- Reduce repetitive manual data entry.
- Submit and verify student information more efficiently.
- Maintain more consistent records.

### Office of Student Affairs

The system will help OSA:

- Maintain standardized student information.
- Manage online submissions through a consistent format.
- Track admission and re-admission records.
- Verify submitted information.
- Reduce inconsistencies in the database.

## Product Goals

The system aims to:

1. **Standardize** student information and submission formats.
2. **Simplify** the dropping and re-admission workflow.
3. **Reduce** repetitive manual data entry.
4. **Guide** students through required processes.
5. **Improve** communication and notifications.
6. **Centralize** relevant student records.
7. **Improve data consistency and integrity**.
8. **Support verification** of submitted student information.

## Desired Outcome

The desired outcome is a system where students, faculty, and OSA can work through a **clear, standardized, and centralized workflow** instead of relying on fragmented forms and processes.

The product should make the process easier to follow for students, less tedious for faculty members, and easier for OSA to manage and maintain.
