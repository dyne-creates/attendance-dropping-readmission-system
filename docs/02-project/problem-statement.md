# Problem Statement

## General Problem

Faculty departments and the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) experience inefficient tracking of student records, course withdrawals, and re-admissions during academic transitions.

The current process involves fragmented information, inconsistent submission formats, extensive manual data entry, and limited guidance for students. These issues can result in mismatched student information, incomplete records, and difficulties in maintaining accurate and consistent data.

Students also experience difficulties completing the required forms and receiving notifications, which can make the re-admission process more complicated.

## Specific Problems

### 1. Inefficient Record Tracking

Faculty departments and OSA have difficulty maintaining and tracking student records across attendance, dropping, admission, and re-admission processes.

### 2. Manual Data Entry

Faculty members need to manually enter large amounts of information into forms, making the process tedious and time-consuming.

### 3. Inconsistent Data Formats

OSA receives information through different submission methods, particularly online and face-to-face processes, resulting in inconsistent data formats.

### 4. Limited Student Guidance

Students who need to undergo re-admission may have little guidance regarding the required steps, forms, and submission process.

### 5. Notification Issues

Students may experience problems retrieving notifications or emails from OSA and potentially from faculty members, making it difficult to stay informed about their applications.

### 6. Mismatched Information

Students may provide information that does not match the records or information provided by faculty, resulting in inconsistencies that require verification.

### 7. Data Integrity Concerns

Inconsistent, incomplete, or unverified information can affect the reliability of student records and may increase the risk of inaccurate or falsified academic records.

## Problem Summary

The existing process lacks a unified and standardized workflow for managing attendance, dropping, admission, and re-admission. This creates difficulties for **students, faculty members, and OSA** in submitting, verifying, tracking, and maintaining student information.
