# Team Working Agreement

This working agreement defines how the team will communicate, conduct meetings, and make decisions throughout the project.

## Communication

1. **Primary Communication Channel**
   - Communication will be conducted through Messenger.

2. **Urgent Matters**
   - For urgent matters, the team will use group messages or face-to-face communication.

3. **Response Time**
   - Team members should respond to messages within **24 hours**.

## Meetings

1. **Meeting Format**
   - Meetings may be conducted online or face-to-face.

2. **Meeting Schedule**
   - The team will coordinate meetings after class or during vacant hours.

3. **Availability**
   - Members who are unavailable must inform the team beforehand.

4. **Documentation**
   - Important information and decisions discussed during meetings must be documented.

## Decision Making

### Disagreements

- When disagreements occur, the team will identify the available options and determine the most feasible solution.

### Product Priorities

- Product priorities will be decided by the **Product Owner**.

### Technical Decisions

- Technical decisions will be made collectively by the **Development Team** based on technical feasibility.

## Team Principles

The team will prioritize:

- Clear communication
- Collaboration
- Accountability
- Feasible decision-making
- Proper documentation
- Respect for each member's responsibilities
