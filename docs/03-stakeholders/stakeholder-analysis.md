# Stakeholder Analysis

## Stakeholder List

| Stakeholder | Type | Relationship | Needs | Concerns | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Office of Student Affairs (OSA)** | Primary | Administrative and regulatory body overseeing student welfare and policy compliance. | Centralized and consistent reports for both face-to-face and online submissions, with reliable data verification. | Disorganized data, untrusted or tampered forms, and complex interfaces with too many buttons. | High |
| **Faculty / Teachers** | Primary | Internal academic managers overseeing class rosters and attendance. | Streamlined workflows that enforce dropping as a prerequisite before re-admission. | Tedious administrative processes and bypassed procedural steps. | High |
| **Students** | Secondary | Users and beneficiaries navigating academic transitions. | Clear guidelines, standardized form fields, and a transparent dropping and re-admission process. | Ambiguous form requirements, such as phone number formats and email fields, and lack of direction. | High |
| **University Policy Office / Regulators** | External | Governing bodies responsible for ensuring adherence to institutional record-keeping rules. | Strict adherence to academic policies and secure student record management. | Non-compliance risks and security vulnerabilities in student data handling. | Medium |

## Stakeholder Needs and Concerns

### Office of Student Affairs (OSA)

**Needs:**
- Centralized student records
- Consistent reports
- Standardized submission formats
- Reliable data verification
- Support for both online and face-to-face submissions

**Concerns:**
- Disorganized data
- Untrusted or tampered forms
- Difficult-to-use interfaces
- Excessive buttons or unnecessary system functions

### Faculty / Teachers

**Needs:**
- Streamlined workflows
- Clear dropping and re-admission procedures
- Efficient student record management
- Reduced administrative workload

**Concerns:**
- Tedious administrative processes
- Repetitive data entry
- Students bypassing required procedural steps

### Students

**Needs:**
- Clear instructions
- Standardized form fields
- Transparent dropping and re-admission process
- Clear submission requirements
- Easy access to relevant information

**Concerns:**
- Unclear form requirements
- Inconsistent formatting requirements
- Lack of guidance
- Difficulty understanding the required procedures

### University Policy Office / Regulators

**Needs:**
- Compliance with institutional policies
- Proper academic record management
- Secure handling of student information

**Concerns:**
- Policy non-compliance
- Security vulnerabilities
- Improper handling of academic records

## Stakeholder Classification

| Stakeholder | Classification | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Office of Student Affairs (OSA) | Primary | High |
| Faculty / Teachers | Primary | High |
| Students | Secondary | High |
| University Policy Office / Regulators | External | Medium |
