# Stakeholder Map

The stakeholder map classifies stakeholders according to their **level of influence** and **level of interest** in the project.

## Influence and Interest Matrix

| | **Low Interest** | **High Interest** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Influence** | **KEEP SATISFIED**<br>University Policy Office / Regulators | **MANAGE CLOSELY**<br>Faculty / Teachers<br>Office of Student Affairs (OSA) |
| **Low Influence** | **MONITOR**<br>No major stakeholder identified | **KEEP INFORMED**<br>Students |

## Stakeholder Categories

### Manage Closely
**Faculty / Teachers**
- High influence
- High interest
- Directly involved in student attendance, dropping, and re-admission processes.

**Office of Student Affairs (OSA)**
- High influence
- High interest
- Responsible for administrative oversight, record management, and verification.

### Keep Satisfied
**University Policy Office / Regulators**
- High influence
- Lower interest in the day-to-day operation of the system
- Responsible for ensuring policy compliance and proper record management.

### Keep Informed
**Students**
- High interest
- Lower influence compared with OSA and faculty members
- Directly affected by the dropping and re-admission processes.

### Monitor
**No major stakeholder identified**
- Low influence
- Low interest
- No major stakeholder currently falls within this category.
